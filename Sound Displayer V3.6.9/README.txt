Sound Displayer V3.6.9
======================

Turns whatever is playing on your computer (or coming in through a microphone)
into live geometry.

START
  Double-click "Start Sound Displayer.bat". It opens the app in its own window.
  (Or open "Sound Displayer.html" in Edge or Chrome.)

LISTEN TO DESKTOP AUDIO
  1. Click "Listen to desktop audio".
  2. In the box that appears, choose "Entire screen".
  3. Switch on "Share system audio" (Chrome calls it "Also share system audio").
  4. Click Share.
  Only the sound is read. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere, and the
  app works with no internet connection.

OTHER SOURCES
  Microphone   listens through your default microphone.

SCENES
  Vortex     nine octaves of sound on the 9-point circle, with the 3-6-9 triangle
  Cymatics   sand settling on a vibrating drum skin
  Rosette    the sound wave drawn as loops, over a 369-point circle

  Symmetry: pick 3, 6 or 9, or press Auto to step through them every
  15 seconds. The switch waits for the next beat so it lands in time.

  The 3, 6, 9 lamps (top right) light when a sound starts, peaks and ends.
  "What am I seeing?" in the app explains everything on screen.

KEYS
  3  6  9      symmetry
  A            auto symmetry on or off
  Space        next scene
  D  M         desktop audio, microphone
  F            full screen
  H            hide or show the controls

SHARE IT
  Send this whole folder (zip it first). Nothing needs installing.
  Works in Edge or Chrome on Windows. On a Mac, desktop audio is limited to
  sound from a browser tab; the microphone works everywhere.

IF NO SOUND SHOWS UP
  Choose "Desktop audio" again, and make sure you pick "Entire screen" and that
  the system audio switch is on. Sharing a single window carries no sound.
